Frequently Asked Questions

1. How does What Movie to Watch Tonight select recommendations?

Our system queries thousands of film records and filters results based on community ratings and popularity thresholds. When you pick a genre, actor, director, or language, we return the top 3 highest-rated movies matching your exact combination.

2. Is this recommendation tool free to use?

Yes, our website is 100% free with no account creation, subscription, or paywalls required.

3. How does the "Surprise Me!" randomizer button work?

The Surprise Me button selects a single top-rated movie at random from our high-rating pool. If you have any active genre, actor, or language filters selected, it will randomly pick a movie that fits those specific criteria.

4. Why are some obscure movies filtered out?

To ensure high recommendation quality, our tool sets a minimum threshold of audience ratings (300+ verified votes). This prevents unrated home videos or low-quality releases from diluting your movie night selection.

5. Can I use this tool on my smartphone or tablet?

Yes! What Movie to Watch Tonight is fully responsive and optimized to work fast across mobile phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers.
